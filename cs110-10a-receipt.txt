## R3 - Discussion and object example
Player movement and direction is depicted through the keyboard and mouse inputs. The character is able to look around 360° by moving the mouse. The character moves by using the WASD keys and Ctrl to sprint around the world. This type of movement capabilities is perfect for Minecraft because of it's sandbox genre and open world qualities. There essentially is no limit to creativity, except the users imagination. 

The use of Unity seems to be the best choice since it allows for multi-platform compatibility. More access across platforms allows for the fans to consume the game at a higher rate than if it is solely a PC game. 

Minecraft was a good choice for a game to research because we are all fans of the game and that lays the foundation for the future. We now know what it feels like to be a fan of a product, and what it looks like when researching how it was produced.
